Getting started

Accounts

QR Chores uses username + password logins.

  • Parent — manage children, chores, QR codes, rewards, today board, history
  • Child — complete their chores via QR, earn points and achievements

There is no public sign-up page. Parent logins are provided for your household. Parents create and manage child accounts under Children.

Log in

  1. Open the app URL
  2. Enter username and password
  3. Parents land on Today; each child lands on Home

Use Log out in the top bar when switching people on a shared phone.

Install as an app

QR Chores works best when you add it to your home screen. It opens full-screen like an app, is easier to find, and feels more natural on a phone (especially for scanning QRs and completing chores).

No App Store or Play Store install — use your browser’s “Add to Home Screen”.

Chrome on Android

  1. Open QR Chores in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right)
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app
  4. Confirm the name, then tap Add / Install

If you don’t see that option, open the site first, then check the menu again. Some phones also show an “Install” banner at the bottom of the page.

Safari on iPhone / iPad

  1. Open QR Chores in Safari (not Chrome or another browser)
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an upward arrow)
  3. Scroll the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Edit the name if you like, then tap Add

“Add to Home Screen” only appears in Safari on iOS. After adding, launch from the home-screen icon instead of the browser.

Parent guide

Today

The Today board shows chores for this calendar day (for your children):

  • Pending — not done yet
  • Done — completed (may also say early / late)
  • Late — still open past due + grace, or completed late

Nav: Today · History · Children · Chores · Rewards

Children

Use Children to add and manage kids who complete chores.

  • First name / last name — shown in lists and the assignee picker
  • Username — login name (not verified as a real email)
  • Password — required when creating; optional when editing
  • Deactivate / Activate — stop or restore that child’s login

Add at least one child before creating chores. Every chore must be assigned to someone. Siblings cannot complete each other’s chores.

Creating and editing chores

  • Title / description — what needs doing
  • Assigned to — required; which child owns this chore and its QR
  • Due time — local wall clock (app timezone, usually America/Chicago)
  • Schedule days — which weekdays it appears
  • Base points — once per day when completed
  • Allow early completion bonus — uncheck if completing early shouldn’t award bonus points
  • Photo policy — required, optional, or none
  • One chore = one stable QR while it’s active
  • Only the assigned child can complete that QR
  • Deactivate stops the QR without wiping history
  • Delete permanently removes chore, completions, and photos

QR codes

  • Each chore has one QR forever while active (not rotated daily)
  • Open QR on a chore to view or download the PNG
  • Use Print for a sticker sheet of all active chores
  • Completing still requires the assigned child to be logged in after scanning

Stickers encode the public site URL. Don’t change that URL after you print, or stickers break.

Rewards

  1. Timing bonuses — extra points for Early / On time / Late (see Timing below). Late may be negative (penalty)
  2. Achievements — badges for streaks, on-time counts, photos, totals, etc.

Awards are calculated on the server when a child completes a chore — the phone never invents points. Points and achievements are per child.

History

Recent completions with timing, points, and proof photos (when provided).

If a chore was completed by mistake, open the 3-dot (⋮) menu next to it on Today or History and select Undo completion to safely revert points and photo.

Child guide

Home

After login you see today’s chores, total points, points change today, and your achievements (Awarded and Not yet awarded). Open a chore on Home, or scan its QR sticker. Only the assigned child can complete it.

Completing a chore

  1. Open the chore on Home, or scan the QR on the sticker
  2. Confirm the name and due time
  3. If a photo is required or optional, take or choose one (JPEG, PNG, or WebP)
  4. Optionally enter a note for parents (e.g., "Running low on cat litter"); an email alert will be sent to your parent(s)
  5. Tap I did it

You’ll see points, a breakdown, and any new achievements.

Rules that matter

  • Once per chore per day — “already done” if you finished earlier
  • Late is OK the same day — after due + grace it still counts (as late)
  • Photos — required chores need a valid image
  • Your chores only — a sibling’s QR won’t complete under your login
  • Achievements — open Achievements for progress and unlocks

Timing: Early, On time, Late

Based on the chore’s due time on the local calendar day (app timezone). Defaults:

  • Early — more than 60 minutes before due
  • On time — from 60 minutes before due through due + 15 minutes grace
  • Late — after due + 15 minutes grace

Example — due 6:00 PM

  • Early — before 5:00 PM
  • On time — 5:00 PM through 6:15 PM
  • Late — after 6:15 PM

Parents set bonus points for each status under Rewards. Completing late the same day is still allowed; base points still apply. The Late bonus can be a negative number (a late penalty), or 0 for no late adjustment.

Photos & privacy

  • Proof photos are stored securely (local disk in dev; cloud storage in production)
  • The database only keeps a reference, not the image bytes
  • Photos are only available to logged-in users through the app — not public links
  • Deleting a chore also removes its proof photos

Points (quick reference)

  • Base points — once per day when the chore is completed
  • Timing bonus — Early / on-time / late amount from Rewards
  • Achievement points — when a new achievement unlocks on complete

Your balance is the sum of the points ledger on the server. Each child has their own balance.

Troubleshooting

  • QR opens wrong site — stickers may use an old public URL; re-print after the URL is final
  • “Wrong account” — log out and log in as a child, not a parent
  • “Not your chore” — log in as the child this chore is assigned to
  • Can’t create a chore — add a child under Children first
  • Can’t complete without photo — photo policy is required; attach JPEG/PNG/WebP
  • Already done — same chore only once per local day
  • Keeps logging out — sessions are cookies; after a server secret change, log in again

Not in the app yet

  • Push or SMS reminders
  • Points shop / redemption
  • Public self-serve signup for new households
  • Native mobile apps
  • Parent “mark done for them” override
